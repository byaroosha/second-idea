## 1. The ceiling (quiet)

What you actually look at when you can't sleep: the bedroom ceiling at 3 a.m., seen from the pillow, with streetlight coming through the blinds.

```
35mm film photograph, slightly underexposed, looking straight up from a pillow at a bedroom ceiling at 3 a.m. The ceiling fills the whole frame: matte plaster in deep plum-grey shadow, one hairline crack, an unlit pendant lamp with a paper shade hanging toward the camera in the top-right corner. Streetlight through window blinds throws a fan of pale sodium-orange stripes diagonally across the lower half, the only warm colour in the image. In the dark upper-left, large off-white capitals in a plain, widely spaced sans-serif read "WIDE AWAKE", stacked on two lines. Coarse film grain, soft halation along the edges of the stripes. Still, private, patient. Square 1:1.
```

## 2. Everyone else is asleep (graphic)

A grid of closed eyes and one open one, in the blue of a phone screen at night. Sleep shows tend toward midnight blue and moons, so a pale cover should also stand out in a podcast app.

```
Two-colour screenprint on warm cream stock, flat solid ink with crisp edges, in lamp black and one cobalt blue. The top two-thirds of the square is a strict grid of twelve eyes, four across and three down. Eleven are closed in sleep, each drawn as a thick black curve like a shut eyelid with three short lashes beneath it. One eye, in the middle row, is wide open: a solid cobalt blue circle with a black pupil, the only blue in the image. Across the bottom third, heavy black condensed capitals spanning the full width of the grid read "WIDE AWAKE" on one line. Slightly uneven ink coverage, light paper grain, a hair of misregistration around the blue. Dry, clear, wry. Square 1:1.
```

## 3. The sheep can't sleep either (funny)

The sheep you were supposed to be counting, sitting up in bed and staring back at you. It also gives the show a mascot.

```
Photograph of a handmade stop-motion miniature set. A needle-felted wool sheep puppet sits bolt upright in a small iron-framed bed, a tomato-red blanket pulled up to its chin, round glass eyes wide open and fixed on the camera. Viewed square-on from the foot of the bed, which sits centred in the lower half of the frame. One small bedside lamp at the left lights faded sage-green wallpaper and throws the long shadow of the sheep's ears up the wall. Large cream capitals, hand-painted and slightly uneven, cross the upper third of the wall: "WIDE AWAKE". Visible wool fibres, hand-stitched blanket hem, props a little too big. Oatmeal, sage and cream, the red blanket the only saturated colour. Deadpan, cosy, sleepless. Square 1:1.
```

## Using them

- **Which generator:** these suit models that take full sentences and can spell, such as GPT Image, Gemini, Ideogram or Flux. Set the aspect ratio to 1:1 in the settings, and turn off any "enhance prompt" or "magic prompt" option, because it rewrites the prompt toward a more generic picture.
- **Lettering:** check the spelling on every result. If it keeps coming out wrong, delete the title sentence, generate the picture alone and add the title yourself in Canva or Figma.
- **Size:** Apple Podcasts asks for artwork between 1400 and 3000 px square, which is larger than most generators produce, so upscale the one you choose.

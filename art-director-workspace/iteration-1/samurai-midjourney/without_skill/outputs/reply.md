What the parameters do, so you can adjust them:

- `--ar 21:9` gives the widescreen film frame. Use `--ar 16:9` for a standard wide shot or `--ar 2:3` for a poster.
- `--style raw` tones down Midjourney's default polish so it reads as a film frame. Remove it for a more painterly look.
- `--s 250` is moderate stylization. Go lower (around 100) for a more literal result, or higher (500+) to let Midjourney take more liberties.

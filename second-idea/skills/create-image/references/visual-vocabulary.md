## Mediums and their tells

Naming a medium is half the job. The other half is naming the physical evidence that it was made that way.

**Printmaking**

| Medium | Tells to mention |
|---|---|
| Linocut | Gouge marks, flat ink, uneven coverage, slight misregistration between colours |
| Woodblock (ukiyo-e) | Visible wood grain in flat colour, fine black keyline, soft graded skies (bokashi) |
| Risograph | Two or three spot inks, grainy halftone, off-register overlaps making a third colour |
| Screenprint | Opaque flat layers, crisp edges, ink overlap at the borders |
| Etching and aquatint | Fine scratched line, grainy tonal areas, plate mark pressed into the paper |
| Lithograph | Greasy crayon texture, soft tonal range |
| Cyanotype | Prussian blue and white only, brushed uneven borders, silhouettes of real objects |
| Letterpress | Type bitten into thick paper, slightly salty ink coverage |

**Painting**

| Medium | Tells to mention |
|---|---|
| Gouache | Opaque, matte, chalky, flat shapes with dry-brush edges |
| Watercolour | Wet-on-wet bleeds, granulating pigment, pooled edges, bare paper as the white |
| Oil, impasto | Thick ridged strokes, palette-knife marks, visible canvas weave |
| Egg tempera and fresco | Dry, pale, finely hatched, slightly dusty surface |
| Ink wash (sumi-e) | Few strokes, graded greys, large areas of bare paper |
| Encaustic | Waxy translucent layers, scraped and fused surface |

**Drawing**

| Medium | Tells to mention |
|---|---|
| Charcoal | Smudged tone, lifted highlights, dust on the paper |
| Graphite | Hatching, construction lines left in, eraser marks |
| Ballpoint | Dense cross-hatching, blue-black sheen, small ink blobs |
| Soft pastel | Powdery blended colour on toned paper, paper showing through |
| Scratchboard | White lines scratched from black, engraving-like hatching |
| Coloured pencil | Waxy layering, visible stroke direction, paper tooth |

**Photographic**

| Medium | Tells to mention |
|---|---|
| Large-format film | Very fine detail, shallow plane of focus, calm tonality |
| 35mm film | Grain, halation around highlights, slightly imperfect exposure |
| Instant film | Soft focus, milky blacks, colour shift, white frame |
| Wet plate or tintype | Shallow focus, swirling edges, chemical streaks, long-exposure stillness |
| Pinhole | Soft everywhere, vignetting, long exposure blur |
| Infrared | White foliage, black skies |
| Long exposure | Water as mist, light trails, ghosted figures |
| Double exposure | Two scenes overlaid, one visible through the other's dark areas |
| Photogram | Objects laid on paper, white silhouettes with soft edges |

**Built and physical**

| Medium | Tells to mention |
|---|---|
| Cut paper and collage | Layered sheets, real shadows between layers, scissor-cut edges, torn fibres |
| Stop-motion puppet or clay | Thumbprints, visible stitching, miniature set, slightly oversized props |
| Embroidery and textile | Raised stitches, puckered cloth, thread sheen, hoop or selvedge |
| Stained glass | Dark lead lines, uneven coloured glass, light passing through |
| Mosaic | Small irregular tiles, grout lines, slight colour variation tile to tile |
| Ceramic and glaze | Pooled glaze, crazing, bare clay foot |
| Diorama or miniature | Tilt-shift depth, handmade scenery, visible scale clues |
| Woodcarving or marquetry | Grain direction, inlaid veneers, tool facets |

**Graphic and digital**

| Medium | Tells to mention |
|---|---|
| Flat vector | Solid filled shapes or line alone, strict limited palette |
| Isometric | Fixed 30-degree projection, no perspective convergence |
| Pixel art | Stated resolution and palette size, dithering |
| Halftone | Visible dot screen, coarse at edges |
| Low-poly or clay render | Faceted or soft matte surfaces, studio light, simple forms |
| Plotter drawing | Single-weight pen lines, mechanical precision with ink wobble |

## Composition

**Where the viewer stands:** bird's-eye, straight down (flat lay), high three-quarter, eye level, low angle, worm's-eye, through a doorway or window, over the shoulder, from inside looking out.

**How close:** extreme close-up on a detail, tight crop that cuts the subject at the frame edge, medium, wide, extreme wide with the subject tiny.

**Where the subject sits:** low in a corner, hard against one edge, off-centre on a third, dead centre and symmetrical (a deliberate choice, strong when everything else is strict), split across a diptych.

**Structure:** strong diagonal, horizon very high or very low, frame within a frame, repetition broken by one exception, leading lines, layered foreground, middle and background, flat frontal with no depth at all.

**Empty space:** say how much and where. "The upper four-fifths is empty sky" is an instruction a model can follow; "minimalist" is not.

**Format follows composition:** tall formats for figures, towers, falling things and solitude; wide formats for horizons, crowds, journeys; square for symmetry, icons and covers.

## Light

**Direction:** front (flat, graphic), side or raking (texture, drama), back (silhouette, rim, glow), top (harsh, institutional), from below (uncanny).

**Quality:** hard with sharp shadows, soft and diffused, dappled through leaves, shafts through haze or dust, reflected and bounced, flat and shadowless.

**Source, named:** north window, single bare bulb, candle, desk lamp, television glow, phone screen, fridge light, sodium streetlamp, neon sign spill, car headlights, fire, moon, overcast sky, low winter sun, the minute after sunset.

**Named setups:** chiaroscuro (one small source, deep surrounding dark), contre-jour (shooting into the light), rim light, silhouette, high-key (nearly all light tones), low-key (nearly all dark tones).

**Weather and air:** fog, mist, haze, rain on glass, dust in the air, heat shimmer, snow light. Air between viewer and subject adds depth and softness.

## Colour

**Schemes**

- Monochrome: one hue in many values
- Duotone: two inks, plus the colour made where they overlap
- Analogous: neighbours on the wheel, calm and unified
- Complementary accent: one dominant colour with a small amount of its opposite
- Muted with a single saturated note

**Proportion matters more than choice.** Name a dominant colour, a supporting one and an accent, and say the accent appears once. Roughly 60/30/10.

**Name colours the way paint is named:** ultramarine, indigo, Prussian blue, cerulean, viridian, sap green, olive, ochre, Naples yellow, cadmium yellow, burnt sienna, raw umber, vermilion, cadmium red, alizarin crimson, oxblood, dusty rose, bone, ivory, Payne's grey, lamp black.

**Palettes that carry a mood**

| Palette | Feeling |
|---|---|
| Indigo, bone, one warm yellow | Night, solitude, watchfulness |
| Ochre, terracotta, dusty pink, sage | Warmth, earth, afternoon |
| Oxblood, cream, black | Formal, weighty, old |
| Mint, lilac, cream, brass | Gentle, slightly odd, domestic |
| Slate, charcoal, one rust orange | Cold morning with one living thing |
| Sun-bleached concrete, pale turquoise, one red | Heat, fatigue, the everyday |
| Mustard and black on off-white | Lively, mid-century, printed |
| Prussian blue and white | Still, archival, cool |

**Value structure:** decide whether the image is mostly dark with small lights, mostly light with small darks, or mid-toned. Squint-level contrast is what reads at thumbnail size.

## Texture and imperfection

Perfection is what reads as machine-made. Pick one or two flaws that belong to the medium.

- Paper: visible fibre, tooth, deckle edge, foxing, fold lines, tape at the corners
- Ink: uneven coverage, misregistration, bleed, offset ghosting
- Paint: brush drag, drips, pentimenti, craquelure, bare canvas at the edges
- Drawing: construction lines, smudges, eraser marks
- Photo: grain, halation, light leaks, dust and scratches, motion blur, missed focus
- Objects: thumbprints, tool marks, chipped glaze, frayed thread, wear at the edges

## Traditions and movements

Each brings a package of composition, colour and line. Name the tradition, then state the two or three qualities you actually want from it.

| Tradition | What it brings |
|---|---|
| Ukiyo-e | Flat colour, bold keyline, cropped asymmetric framing, graded skies |
| Persian and Mughal miniature | Jewel colours, flattened stacked space, fine pattern, ornate borders |
| Illuminated manuscript | Gold leaf, decorated initials, margin creatures, vellum |
| Byzantine icon | Gold ground, frontal figures, stylised drapery |
| Dutch Golden Age still life | Dark ground, one window light, lavish surfaces, quiet symbolism |
| Romantic landscape | Vast nature, tiny figure, weather as emotion |
| Impressionism | Broken colour, light over detail, visible strokes |
| Pointillism | Dots of pure colour mixing in the eye |
| Art Nouveau | Whiplash curves, botanical ornament, decorative borders |
| Vienna Secession | Flat pattern, gold, geometry with ornament |
| Expressionism | Distorted form, harsh colour, anxious line |
| Fauvism | Arbitrary saturated colour, simplified shapes |
| Cubism | Several viewpoints at once, faceted planes, muted palette |
| Surrealism | Dream logic rendered with sober realism |
| Constructivism | Diagonals, red, black and cream, photomontage, bold type |
| Bauhaus | Primary colours, geometry, grid, function |
| De Stijl | Black grid, primary rectangles, white space |
| Art Deco | Symmetry, sunbursts, stepped forms, metallics |
| WPA and travel posters | Flat screenprinted colour, simplified landscape, block lettering |
| Mid-century modern illustration | Cut shapes, limited spot colour, texture, wit |
| Polish poster school | Painterly metaphor, hand lettering, one strange central image |
| Swiss style | Grid, sans-serif, asymmetry, photography with flat colour |
| Psychedelic poster | Vibrating complementary colours, melting lettering |
| Pop Art | Halftone dots, flat brights, commercial imagery |
| Op Art | Optical vibration from repeated geometry |
| Colour field | Large soft-edged areas of colour, nothing else |
| Film noir | Hard shadows, venetian-blind light, wet streets |
| Pictorialism | Soft-focus, painterly early photography |
| New Topographics | Deadpan photographs of ordinary built landscape |
| Memphis | Clashing pattern, squiggles, laminate colours |
| Folk and vernacular art | Flat pattern, symmetry, hand-painted line; name the specific tradition and treat it with respect |

## Mood into visible things

Models render objects, light and colour. Convert the mood word before writing.

| Mood | What to put in the frame |
|---|---|
| Lonely | Small subject, wide empty frame, cool palette with one warm point, distance |
| Tender | Close framing, soft side light, warm muted colour, hands or small gestures |
| Tense | Tight crop, tilted horizon, hard side light, something about to fall or touch |
| Calm | Horizontals, large simple shapes, analogous colour, soft even light, lots of space |
| Joyful | Diagonals, saturated spot colour, overlapping shapes, movement caught mid-air |
| Nostalgic | Faded dyes, warm halation, soft edges, period objects, late afternoon light |
| Uncanny | Strict symmetry, flat frontal light, ordinary things slightly wrong, shadows missing where they should fall |
| Monumental | Low viewpoint, a tiny figure for scale, simple heavy forms, haze with distance |
| Dreamlike | Soft focus, floating objects, mixed scales, dusk colour, no clear light source |
| Melancholy | Overcast light, muted palette, a figure turned away, rain on glass |
| Playful | Odd proportions, bright limited palette, visible hand of the maker, a small joke in a corner |
| Sacred | Vertical format, light from above, gold or candle, symmetry, stillness |
| Eerie | Fog, a single distant light, empty places built for crowds, cold green or sodium yellow |
| Energetic | Motion blur, strong diagonals, cropped limbs, high contrast, repeated shapes |
